# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and professional project community.

Contributors must communicate constructively, respect differing experiences, avoid harassment and discrimination, protect private information, and keep technical debate focused on the work. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation that violate these expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub contact channel. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues. Enforcement decisions will prioritize safety, proportionality, and the health of the project community.
